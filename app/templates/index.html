Methodology

How the Model Works

01

Data Collection

Historical daily price data from 550+ price reporting centres managed by the Department of Consumer Affairs.

02

Feature Engineering

Seasonality, trend decomposition, crop sowing cycles, and buffer stock data are encoded as model features.

03

LSTM Training

PyTorch Long Short-Term Memory networks learn temporal dependencies in price sequences across commodities.

04

Price Forecast

The trained model generates forward predictions for wholesale prices, which are converted to retail estimates.

About the Project

AI-Powered Market Intelligence for Indian Agriculture

This system was developed to support the Department of Consumer Affairs in strategic market interventions for essential food commodities. The department monitors daily prices of 22 commodities and maintains buffer stocks of pulses and onion.

The model improves upon traditional ARIMA-based economic models by leveraging deep learning to capture complex non-linear temporal patterns, seasonal dependencies, and multi-commodity correlations.

Flask PyTorch Pandas NumPy Scikit-learn LSTM

Model Limitations

  • 📅 Predictions beyond 15 days decrease in reliability
  • 🌧 Unexpected weather events are not captured in historical data
  • 📊 Model depends on historical price trends; structural market shifts may affect output
  • ⚠ Agricultural commodity prices are inherently volatile in the long term
